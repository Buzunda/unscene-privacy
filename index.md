# Unscene privacy policy

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

Unscene is a browser extension that hides stories about people you choose not to see. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. This policy explains everything it stores and every request it makes.

## What Unscene stores, and where

- **Your list and settings.** The people you add, their nicknames, how their stories should look, and your per-site choices. These are saved in your browser's extension storage. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome copies them to your other computers through your Google account. Unscene's developer never receives them.
- **Good news headlines.** If you turn on Good news mode, the day's headlines (title, link and image address) are saved in your browser's local storage.
- **Hidden-item counts.** The number of items hidden on each open tab, for the toolbar badge. These are kept only while Chrome is running.

Removing Unscene deletes this data from your browser.

## What Unscene reads

To find the names on your list, Unscene reads the text of the pages you visit. This happens entirely on your computer. Nothing about the pages you visit, or what was hidden on them, is sent anywhere or kept after you leave the page.

When you click the toolbar icon, Unscene reads the current tab's address, to show its count and per-site switch.

## Requests Unscene makes

Unscene contacts only one site, and only if you turn on Good news mode for someone:

- **Daily headlines.** Once a day, it downloads the latest public posts from Good News Network (goodnewsnetwork.org). The request carries no cookies and contains nothing about you, your list or the pages you visit.
- **Card photos.** Headline photos load from goodnewsnetwork.org without a referrer, so Good News Network can't tell which site the card appeared on. Like any image from another site, loading it does tell their server that someone loaded it, and, if your browser allows third-party cookies, may carry cookies Good News Network has set.
- **Opening an article.** Clicking a headline opens the article on goodnewsnetwork.org in a new tab, as an ordinary visit.

In Replace mode you can choose your own pictures by web address. Unscene loads them from that address, without a referrer.

The "Fill these in with an AI chat" feature only copies a prompt to your clipboard. Unscene doesn't contact any AI service.

## What Unscene never does

- It doesn't collect, sell or share personal data.
- It doesn't track your browsing.
- It doesn't run code downloaded from the internet.

## Third parties

Good News Network is an independent site with its own privacy policy. Unscene isn't affiliated with it.
